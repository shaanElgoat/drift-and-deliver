HOW TO PLAY

📦 Grab cargoCrates parachute in all over the city (watch for the yellow rings). Drive through one to load it. You carry up to 3.
🏁 Bank itDrive into your team's DROP ZONE to add your crates to your depot. Your score is how many crates sit in your depot.
🦝 Raid themPark in the enemy drop zone to steal crates from their pile, then race them home. Defenders sitting in their zone slow your looting.
💥 Ram & snatchSlam into an enemy carrying cargo to snatch a crate straight onto your roof. Boost-rams knock everything loose!
📣 HonkYour horn is a shockwave: it shoves nearby enemies and shakes a crate off any carrier. Recharges in 6s.
⚡ SupersEvery van has a Super that charges as you play — Magnet, Nitro, Quake or Shield. When the meter glows, hit SUPER.
👥 Party upTap INVITE and send the link to friends. Everyone in your party drives together — bots fill any empty seats.
🏆 ClimbWin to earn trophies and climb from Bronze to Legend — bots get smarter as you rise. Level up for new vans and cosmetics, and clear daily quests.
🌀 Drift-turboHold drift through corners. Sparks turn blue, then orange — let go for a mini-turbo. Drifting also refills boost.
Keyboard: WASD/arrows drive · Space drift · Shift boost · E honk · Q Super · R unstick · Esc pause   Phone: auto-gas, slide the left pad to steer, buttons on the right. Gamepad works too. Mega drops land at the fountain every minute. First to the target wins — or most crates when time runs out.
